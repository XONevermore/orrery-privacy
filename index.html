Orrery — Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026

Orrery shows the solar system as it really is, drawn on your phone. It is built so that nothing about you needs to leave it.

What Orrery collects

Orrery itself collects nothing about you. It has no accounts and no servers of its own, it does not store, sell or share personal information, and it does not use your precise location, contacts, photos or microphone. The only data that leaves your device is described under “Ads”, “Analytics” and “Notifications” below, and it goes to Google (and, for ads, to Yandex), not to us.

What stays on your device

Your settings — language, time speed, graphics quality and the like — and whether Orrery Pro is unlocked are kept on your phone only, so the app opens the way you left it. Deleting the app deletes them.

If you type what you weigh in Weigh yourself, that number is kept on your phone only, to work out your weight on other worlds; it is never sent anywhere.

Sky alerts, if you turn them on, are notifications that Orrery schedules on your phone itself, for eclipses, planets at their best, meteor showers and the like. The dates are worked out on your phone from the positions of the planets that Orrery already carries; nothing about you or about the alerts is sent to Orrery or to anyone else, and you choose whether to allow them, in the sheet that offers them or in Settings, and can turn them off or take the permission back at any time in Android's settings.

What the app downloads

To show photographs, recordings and surface maps, Orrery downloads public files from NASA and the U.S. Geological Survey (for example images.nasa.gov, trek.nasa.gov, gibs.earthdata.nasa.gov). These requests carry nothing about you beyond what any web request does — your device's IP address, seen by the server that answers. Orrery keeps the files it downloads on your device so it need not fetch them again.

Purchases

Orrery Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; Orrery never sees your payment details or your Google Account. The app asks Google Play only whether Pro is unlocked. Google's own privacy policy covers the purchase: policies.google.com/privacy.

Ads

Orrery shows ads to people who have not bought Orrery Pro: a full-screen ad now and then at a natural pause (never more than one every three minutes), one when you come back to the app after several hours away, and ads you may choose to watch — to open Pro features for two hours, to fly Dark Side for a day, or for a second life in a Dark Side match. They are served by Google AdMob and by the Yandex Advertising Network (Yandex serves the ads in Russia, where Google does not, and fills in elsewhere when Google has no ad). To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, these networks may collect your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers, your approximate location (from your IP address), how you interact with the ads and the app, and crash and diagnostic information. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) you are asked for your consent on first launch and can change it later. You can reset or turn off personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Orrery Pro removes the ads: no ad is requested or shown to someone who has it. How Google uses this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy; how Yandex does, at yandex.com/legal/confidential. Orrery does not let Yandex use your location.

Analytics

For people who have not bought Orrery Pro, Orrery uses Google Analytics for Firebase to learn how the app is used, so it can be made better: when it is opened, how long a session lasts, which screens are visited, the kind of phone and version of Android, and the country (worked out from the IP address, which Google does not keep). Google gives each installation a random identifier; it is not your name, your email or your Google Account, and it is not shared with us beyond these counts. Analytics starts only after the same consent as the ads, where the law requires it, and stops if you withdraw it. Orrery Pro turns it off: nothing is counted for someone who has it. How Google handles this data is described at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Notifications

Now and then Orrery may send a short announcement, such as news of a new feature, to everyone or to the people who use it in one language. These are delivered by Google’s Firebase Cloud Messaging. To receive them, your installation gets an anonymous push token from Firebase, which is subscribed to two topics: “all” and the app’s language (for example “lang_ru”). The token and the topics are held by Google, and are used only to deliver these announcements; they are not your name, email or Google Account, and Orrery has no server of its own that sees them. Nothing else about you is sent. You can turn this off in Orrery’s Settings under News and updates, which unsubscribes and deletes the token, or turn off notifications for Orrery in Android’s settings. On Android 13 and later Orrery asks for permission to show notifications once, and does not ask again if you say no.

Sharing

Sharing a still uses the system share sheet, and the image goes only where you send it.

Children

Orrery is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here and in the app, with its date.
